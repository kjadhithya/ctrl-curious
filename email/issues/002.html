>  Today, in brief
A day after Config India, Figma's finance chief said the company is willing to accept thinner margins to keep funding AI, a sign that metered agents and AI credits are a lasting part of the toolkit. Figma also shared new survey data from India, where builders are more excited than their US and European peers about AI changing their work. Apple, meanwhile, pulled its redesigned Health app from the iOS 27.2 beta, a reminder that even big platform redesigns slip late. On the craft side, The Gradient argues that generative UI is only as good as the design system behind it, and UX Collective makes the case that AI-generated UI is fast design, not UX.
