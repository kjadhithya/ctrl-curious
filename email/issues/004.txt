Today, in brief
Apple's Pro and tell event on 28 October is expected to bring a redesigned MacBook Pro with an OLED touchscreen and Dynamic Island (https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/19/apple-is-launching-major-new-products-next-week-heres-whats-coming/), which would make touch a real input for Mac interfaces. Smaller but just as instructive, Google's Play Store now shows time or size remaining on downloads instead of a percentage (https://9to5google.com/2026/10/19/october-2026-google-system-updates/). Two pieces sharpen the craft: Patrick Neeman argues critiques should run on cited principles and evidence, not taste (https://uxdesign.cc/taste-is-not-a-design-methodology-e02b37aea148), and SAP's Arin Bhowmick shows how to answer a CFO in payback and conversion terms (https://uxdesign.cc/what-i-told-the-cfo-when-he-asked-why-design-matters-ceadb07c929e). And a week after Config India, Nil Thyrion asks designers to own the experience people actually receive, not stop at handoff (https://uxdesign.cc/beyond-the-canvas-03fce0bcbf7b).
