Today, in brief
Google is putting a checkpoint into AI generation: Gemini Notebook now lets you review and edit the outline (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/) before it builds slides or a video, a pattern worth borrowing for any generative feature you design. On the craft side, CSS random() has shipped in Safari and is rolling out in Chrome (https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2026/10/embracing-playful-imperfection-css-random-function/), so controlled imperfection no longer needs JavaScript. Redaction, the serif MCKL drew from the type of American court papers (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Redaction), is now free on Google Fonts with its six degraded cuts. And Apple's Designers of Tomorrow showcase opened in Paris (https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/20/apples-designers-of-tomorrow-highlights-creative-projects-from-emerging-designers) with a jury that included its VP of industrial design.
