Today, in brief
Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet (https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-takes-on-chatgpt-dots-and-meta-muse-with-claude-duet/), a proactive agent that sends a morning brief, keeps working in the background and asks before it sends, pays, signs or deletes anything. Early testers like Claire Vo find it polished but too quiet (https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/claude-duet-is-beautiful-i-still) to become a habit, and Jakob Nielsen's latest roundup (https://jakobnielsenphd.substack.com/p/ux-roundup-20261019) adds a rule every agent needs: a reliable way to stop a run without losing finished work. Apple Music unveiled a new identity with a logo shaped by sound (https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/21/apple-music-unveils-new-design-rebrand-with-logo-made-by-sound/), arriving with iOS 27.2 next week. And today's case study is the VS Code team's modern UI rollout (https://code.visualstudio.com/blogs/2026/10/15/modern-ui), a lesson in refreshing a tool people use all day without breaking their muscle memory.
