Today, in brief
Apple's first foldable goes on sale today, and reviewers agree the weak spot is software from other people: Engadget's review praises the multitasking (https://www.engadget.com/2292333/apple-iphone-duo-review/) but finds Telegram and Gmail floating in dead space on the inner screen. The apps that work, like Slack, Netflix and Spotify, lean on two panes, tabletop splits and side controls (https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/ive-been-testing-dozens-of-iphone-duo-apps-heres-my-top-15-picks-so-far), much as Apple's own apps do (https://macrumors.com/2026/10/20/apple-apps-iphone-duo) with sidebars, dual panes and a dock on the right. Even Apple Books is moving to standard Liquid Glass controls (https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/ios-27-2-introduces-design-changes-for-these-apple-apps/) in iOS 27.2, likely so they reposition on the Duo. And Framer opened its canvas to outside agents (https://www.framer.com/updates/framer-mcp), so Claude, Codex or Cursor can now build and edit your Framer site directly.
