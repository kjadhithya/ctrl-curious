Today, in brief
Apple's Wednesday event should bring the first touchscreen Mac, and Mark Gurman reports that macOS will switch its controls depending on whether you last touched the screen or the trackpad (https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/25/macbook-pro-with-touchscreen-on-wednesday/), with menus opening around your finger. On the iPhone Duo, Apple is weighing letting websites draw under Safari's side controls (https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/25/apple-wants-to-fix-safari-on-iphone-duo/) because too many sites mishandle the screen's edges, while Flipboard went the other way and turned stories into book pages (https://ppc.land/flipboard-redesigns-its-news-app-as-a-two-page-book-for-iphone-duo/) on the inner screen. NN/g's survey of 521 practitioners finds AI speeds up most UX tasks but helps least with work involving users (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-in-ux-survey-26/), and checking its output is the top complaint. And for something to fix today, Vitaly Friedman's roundup of accordion icon research (https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2026/10/accordion-icons-which-work-best/) makes the case for a plain caret.
