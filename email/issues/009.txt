Today, in brief
Apple opened its launch week with an OLED iPad mini that is thinner, IP67-rated and now starts at $699 (https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/26/apple-announces-ipad-mini-with-oled-display/), and teased more to come (https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/26/apple-teases-exciting-week-ahead/) before Wednesday, when touchscreen MacBook Pros are expected. Google, meanwhile, gave Gemini Live a redesigned plus button that makes it easier to add photos and files (https://9to5google.com/2026/10/26/gemini-live-plus-redesign/). For the craft, Jakob Nielsen's latest roundup sets out rules for proactive AI that respect a user's train of thought (https://www.uxtigers.com/post/ux-roundup-20261026), and this week's UX Collective argues the race that matters is winning stakeholder alignment, not shipping faster (https://newsletter.uxdesign.cc/p/why-your-team-is-telling-the-wrong). And Stéphanie Walter's weekly picks make a sharp case for fixing everyday accessibility before chasing gadgets (https://stephaniewalter.design/blog/pixels-of-the-week-october-25-2026/).
