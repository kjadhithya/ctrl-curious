>  Decision toolkit
Let the Mac translate touch, or design for it?
macOS now turns taps into clicks, so most apps work untouched. The call is where that's enough.
Let it translate when
›  The screen is mostly standard system controls
›  Precise work stays on the trackpad anyway
›  Every action already has a click route
 
Design for touch when
›  People drag, draw or scrub on a canvas
›  Actions only appear on hover
›  Custom controls are smaller than a fingertip
Seen in the wild
Pixelmator Pro
Apple rebuilt it so you draw, paint and select straight on the canvas.
Rule of thumb  If a finger has to do something a click can't, design it; otherwise let macOS translate.
