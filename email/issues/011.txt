Today, in brief
Apple's new MacBook Pro turns touches into clicks (https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/) so most apps work on day one, and early hands-ons show a desktop that stays a desktop, with menu bar items that grow when tapped (https://gizmodo.com/macos-touchscreen-macbook-pro-2000831715). The real homework is Apple's Touch on Mac guidance (https://developer.apple.com/touch-on-mac/): hit targets, hover and controls that expand under a finger.
