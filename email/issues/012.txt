Today, in brief
GitHub Design has open-sourced Replica (https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-introducing-replica-an-open-source-ai-prototyping-tool/), a prototyping tool that works from your team's own components and approved guidance, so a teammate's prompt produces something that looks like your product instead of a generic template. The first hands-on reviews of Apple's touch Mac show how lightly the interface adapts: macOS spaces out menu items when it senses a finger (https://www.macstories.net/stories/macbook-pro-with-touch-a-mac-for-the-ipad-generation/), and the window buttons grow slightly under a fingertip (https://www.engadget.com/2300023/apples-first-touchscreen-macbook-pro-feels-both-familiar-and-radically-improved/), while most controls stay as they were. Baymard's survey of 2,273 shoppers draws a clear line for AI features: chatbots for comparing and researching, people for problems with orders (https://baymard.com/research-articles/ai-engagement-quantitative-ux-insights-2026). And Google's Gemini app now puts every setting on one page (https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/10/gemini-android-app-redesign-update.html), a pattern worth weighing for any AI product whose settings have sprawled.
