Today, in brief
NN/g's Tanner Kohler read the AI-native development playbooks from the big labs and found they keep UX only as background context, so he lays out where user research drops out (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-agentic-software-factories/) and how designers can win it back, from review triggers to owning the context files. His colleague Raluca Budiu argues the field now covers agents acting for people as well as the people themselves (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-vs-experience-design/), with handoffs between the two as the place things break. On the craft side, Karl Koch shows what a trustworthy undo for AI edits (https://karlkoch.me/writing/on-giving-people-an-undo-button-in-ai-products) needs, and Den Odell's speed test finds Ionic took over a second to open a 249-item country picker (https://denodell.com/blog/design-systems-should-be-fast-by-default). Before Wednesday, note that Figma's code layers stop being free on paid plans after 4 November (https://forum.figma.com/product-updates-3/here-s-everything-from-config-india-2026-59008).
