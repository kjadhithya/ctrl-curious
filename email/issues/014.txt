Today, in brief
Canva has released Affinity for iPad free for individuals (https://www.creativeboom.com/tools/behold-affinity-for-ipad-the-app-that-wants-to-retire-your-four-other-apps/), with sketch, vector, photo and layout work in one touch-first file, days after shipping Brush, a model that edits existing layouts (https://thenewstack.io/canva-brush-design-editing) instead of generating flat images. Framer's rebuilt agent harness (https://www.framer.com/updates/harness-2) now edits components, CMS, forms and branches in one pass, and gives outside agents the same reach through MCP. The craft lesson of the day comes from Victor Yocco, who shows why approving AI summaries leaves people unable to explain them (https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2026/11/the-generation-effect-in-ai-workflows-why-reviewing-causes-forgetting.php) and how to design friction that makes oversight stick. And X's head of design Benji Taylor has open-sourced Stageshow (https://benji.org/stageshow), a skill that teaches coding agents to make animated product demos that don't look fake.
