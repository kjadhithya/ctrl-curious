Today, in brief
Safari 27.3 beta can now paint a dark version of any light website (https://webkit.org/blog/18541/introducing-adaptive-dark-mode/) for people who use dark mode, and the site is never told, so teams that never shipped a dark theme should look at what Safari makes of theirs; today's brief covers how. The same Apple beta brings back the redesigned Health app (https://www.apple.com/in/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/), rebuilt around daily insights and a single Health Age comparison. Canva says it will cut or change about 1,270 roles (https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-04/canva-restructure-job-losses/107344304) and return to smaller product teams as growth slows for the first time in a decade. Closer to your own work, the Design Systems Collective argues for testing AI on a few hundred cases from your own system (https://designsystemscollective.substack.com/p/fast-and-wrong-is-still-wrong), and Shopify's Derek Briggs explains on Dive Club how he raises the bar for craft (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4IW73e6tE4) across a big team.
