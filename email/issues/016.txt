Today, in brief
Apple has opened Siri AI to every developer (https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=gdk2umrf) in the 27.3 betas, so an app's content and actions can now be reached by voice and personal context through App Intents, which turns your intents into an interface of their own. Figma now lets the agent and Make read and create files in Google Drive (https://forum.figma.com/product-updates-3/connect-google-drive-to-the-figma-agent-and-make-59533) on every plan, and Framer's agent can now build and analyse A/B tests in Convert (https://www.framer.com/updates/convert-update). Two teams show what building your own AI tools looks like: Zalando made a prototyping environment on its design system (https://www.intodesignsystems.com/blog/zalando-design-system-ai-prototyping-tool) after Figma Make came out off-brand, and GOV.UK built a test runner for content no one can read before it is generated (https://designnotes.blog.gov.uk/2026/11/03/creating-tools-for-scaling-generated-content/), today's case study.
