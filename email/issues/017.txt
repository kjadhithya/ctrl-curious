Today, in brief
YouTube's biggest navigation change in about a decade is reaching phones: the Subscriptions tab is gone from the bottom bar (https://9to5google.com/2026/11/05/youtube-app-redesign-subscriptions-tab/), now a swipe away inside Home, and early reaction is harsh, a reminder of what it costs to move a habit people rely on. Figma's third-quarter results put numbers on AI in design work: 65% of customers paying over $10K a year use the Figma agent weekly (https://www.stocktitan.net/news/FIG/figma-announces-third-quarter-2026-financial-xwr5ldww6g5v.html), and MCP use tripled in a quarter. Apple now lets apps show a retention message inside the App Store cancellation flow (https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=fk9ps3gv), which puts a new screen, and a new ethical line, in front of subscription designers. And two teams share what they learned building: Lovable on why it pulled autonomy back from its agent (https://lovable.dev/blog/how-we-built-lovable-and-why-we-keep-rebuilding-it), Discord on the small craft rules behind its new video player (https://discord.com/blog/modernizing-the-video-playback-experience-at-discord).
