>  Today, in brief
Adobe MAX opens today in Miami Beach, and Adobe's pitch going in is that the skill that matters next is creative direction rather than better prompting, with its Firefly assistant working across the Creative Cloud apps and reaching into ChatGPT, Claude and Slack. Jakob Nielsen reads a new study in which one standard AI prompt beat both amateurs and professional creatives, and buyers couldn't tell whose work was whose, a hard prompt for anyone whose value rests on taste. On the interface side, Google is testing a Chrome for Android settings page with no Basics and Advanced labels, and Google Messages moves search to the bottom of the screen for one-handed use. And the Design Systems Collective argues that most of a system team's job is now deciding what stays out of the memory agents share.
>  Tool of the day
Relume Studio
Relume released Studio on 3 November to make on-brand images that stay consistent: Styles lock the look, Characters lock the people from a few uploaded photos, Scenes lock a place from one photo, and BrandKits set up all three from a single URL. It is best at keeping the same people, places and style across every page of a marketing site, with images going straight into a Relume site without exporting or renaming files.

>  Tool to master
Figma's AI image tools: erase, isolate and expand without leaving the file
Since 6 November, clicking an object in an image makes Figma select it for you, so there is no more tracing around it by hand. That makes the image tools inside Figma quick enough to replace a round trip to a photo editor for most interface and slide work.
1
Pull out or remove one thing. Select the image and choose Select area in the secondary toolbar (in FigJam it sits under AI tools). Click the object on the macOS desktop app, or drag the lasso anywhere else, then choose Erase to remove it or Isolate to lift it onto its own layer.
2
Fit an image to a new frame. Choose More, then Expand, drag the blue handles or pick an aspect ratio from the dropdown, and click the blue check: useful when a hero shot has to work at desktop, mobile and social sizes.
3
Clean up a batch at once. Select several images and use Remove background, or More then Boost resolution for low-res screenshots and stock; image jobs run side by side and you can keep working, even in another frame.
4
Change a detail with words. Choose Edit with prompt, pick a model, add a reference image with the plus button if you have one, and send with Command and Enter.
 Our take  You need edit access to the file, and the one-click selection is on the macOS desktop app only. Each action spends AI credits: removing a background costs 1 to 5 per image and erase, isolate, expand or boost cost 5 to 10, against a monthly allowance of 3,000 for a Professional full seat and 500 for Starter, Dev, Collab and View seats. Our advice: use Isolate on product shots before handoff, so engineers get a clean layer instead of a flattened photo.
