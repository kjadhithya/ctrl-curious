Today, in brief
Adobe MAX opens today in Miami Beach, and Adobe's pitch going in is that the skill that matters next is creative direction rather than better prompting (https://www.theneuron.ai/news/adobe-says-the-next-ai-skill-isnt-better-prompting-its-creative-direction/), with its Firefly assistant working across the Creative Cloud apps and reaching into ChatGPT, Claude and Slack. Jakob Nielsen reads a new study in which one standard AI prompt beat both amateurs and professional creatives (https://www.uxtigers.com/post/ux-roundup-20261109), and buyers couldn't tell whose work was whose, a hard prompt for anyone whose value rests on taste. On the interface side, Google is testing a Chrome for Android settings page with no Basics and Advanced labels (https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/11/google-is-giving-chrome-for-android-a-fresh-set-of-colorful-icons.html), and Google Messages moves search to the bottom of the screen (https://9to5google.com/2026/11/07/google-messages-pinned-chats-redesign/) for one-handed use. And the Design Systems Collective argues that most of a system team's job is now deciding what stays out (https://designsystemscollective.substack.com/p/most-of-the-job-is-deciding-what) of the memory agents share.
