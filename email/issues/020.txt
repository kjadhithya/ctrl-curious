Today, in brief
At Adobe MAX yesterday, Adobe said outside AI agents will be able to work on native Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and After Effects files (https://www.creativeboom.com/news/adobe-max-2026-all-the-big-announcements/) with layers left editable, and showed Firefly Builder, which turns a prompt into a clickable, on-brand prototype (https://www.creativebloq.com/news/adobes-firefly-is-now-a-creative-studio-and-it-means-ai-is-everything-everywhere-all-at-once); it is in private beta, so its design system claims are still to be tested. Figma Motion now has physics-based springs you can save to your design system (https://www.figma.com/release-notes/?title=new-figma-motion-updates-physics-based-springs-style-and-lottie-export-previews), and Framer has rebuilt the harness around its own agent (https://www.framer.com/updates/harness-2) so changes appear while the model is still working; today's brief explains why that layer shapes what your users can do. If you work on a design system, a count of 451 job ads shows AI now appears in about two thirds of senior and lead roles (https://www.intodesignsystems.com/blog/ai-skills-design-system-jobs). And Eric Bailey shows how to make AI chat usable with a screen reader (https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2026/11/making-immersive-llm-chat-experiences-more-accessible/), from one live region to a five-second loading rhythm.
