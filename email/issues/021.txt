Today, in brief
Anthropic now lets partner apps open inside Claude (https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool), full screen or beside the chat, with Canva, Webflow and Adobe in the first set and Figma building one, so a product's own interface can now live inside someone else's AI app. At Adobe MAX, Project Link Up (https://www.creativebloq.com/features/adobe-max-2026-sneaks-project-link-up-is-making-design-playful-again) showed vector art that re-fits its perspective, scale and style to whatever scene you drop it into, and Webflow added native modals and popovers (https://webflow.com/updates/new-modal-and-popover-elements). Underneath runs a shift Case Study Club tracks this week (https://www.casestudy.club/issues/022/): teams such as Help Scout building their own prototyping tools on their design systems, which only works once the library is legible to an agent (https://modeinspect.com/blog/design-system-audit). Today's case study shows Adobe's team making the prompt optional (https://adobe.design/ideas/behind-the-design-concept-to-vector-in-adobe-illustrator) in Illustrator's Concept to Vector.
