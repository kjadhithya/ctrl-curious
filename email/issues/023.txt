Today, in brief
When coding agents make shipping cheap, teams release first and learn later, so NN/g's Tony Alicea proposes a research section written into every feature spec (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/research-in-the-loop/), with signs of success, signs of harm and a trigger for talking to people. Adobe's designers explain how they are moving Photoshop onto Spectrum without disrupting people's habits (https://adobe.design/ideas/behind-the-design-adobe-photoshop-modernization), including mapping Spectrum's three themes onto Photoshop's four. Before your team generates anything client-facing in Firefly, read why Adobe's commercial-use assurances stop at its own models (https://www.creativebloq.com/news/i-trusted-adobe-firefly-because-it-was-the-safe-ai-option-now-im-not-so-sure). And if you have a talk in you, Figma's call for Config 2027 speakers closes today (https://config.figma.com/apply-to-speak-2027/).
