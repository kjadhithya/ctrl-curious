Today, in brief
Canva now lets Free and Pro users hide its generative AI features with one setting (https://www.trendingtopics.eu/canva-ai-off-switch-layoffs/), a control only team admins had before, which is a useful precedent if your product is adding AI everywhere by default. Bitwarden's redesign is a lesson in naming: it swapped collections for shared folders (https://bitwarden.com/blog/2026-design-update/) after a month-long public beta, because people didn't understand the old words. Two pieces show where AI speed is leaving gaps: Level Access found that testing hasn't sped up the way design and build have (https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2026/11/ai-sped-up-build-how-can-accessibility-testing-keep-up/), and zeroheight lists what to fix so agents can read your design system docs (https://zeroheight.com/blog/fixing-a-low-machine-readability-score/). For the weekend, a new free skill called craft (https://github.com/diego3g/craft) makes coding agents write a brief and list every state before they draw a screen.
