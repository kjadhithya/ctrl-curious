>  Decision toolkit
Hand a design task to AI, or keep a person on it?
Use Nielsen's verification ladder, then ask whether the feature should think with people or for them.
Hand it to AI when
›  A checklist can judge the result in minutes
›  You want many options and will test to choose
›  Your guidelines are written down
 
Keep a person on it when
›  Only users or the market can judge it
›  Doing it builds your judgement, as with synthesis
›  It decides what's worth doing or aligns the team
Seen in the wild
Baymard UX-Ray
95% agreement with human auditors, because it is built on human-mapped data.
Rule of thumb  If you can't say how the output gets judged, keep a person on it.
