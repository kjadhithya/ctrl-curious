Today, in brief
Framer's agent can now run on a schedule or on every publish and act in Slack, Notion and Ahrefs (https://www.framer.com/updates/automations-connectors-slack), and Figma's agent is now available in Dev Mode (https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/37998629035799-Work-with-the-Figma-agent-in-design-files), where developers can ask for code snippets and version diffs without touching the file. Apple's iOS 27.3 moves Calendar's search and new-event buttons to the bottom of the screen (https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/25/ios-27-3-introduces-design-changes-for-these-apple-apps/) for one-handed reach, a small change worth copying. Jakob Nielsen argues that AI takes over UX tasks in the order their results can be checked (https://www.uxtigers.com/post/ai-ux-progress-rate), the idea behind today's decision toolkit. And Microsoft's AI studio explains why its Autopilot agent is drawn as a line, not a mascot (https://microsoft.design/articles/the-art-of-playing-a-supporting-role/).
