>  Today, in brief
Figma's AI agent is now generally available, with team guidelines it follows and threads your colleagues can see. >  Tool of the day
Figma agent
An AI agent inside Figma design files that generates, remixes and automates design tasks, and can follow your team's conventions through shareable skills. Best for the repetitive work around a design: variants, clean-up and first drafts you then refine.

>  Tool to master
Build from your own files in Figma Make
Figma Make now takes around 30 file types as prompt context, from designs and React components to spreadsheets and video. A few ways to put it to work:
1
Turn a sample API response (JSON) into a working interface to test with real shapes of data.
2
Reuse your existing React components instead of having Make invent new ones.
3
Convert a static HTML and CSS page into a modular, interactive prototype.
4
Mind the limits: up to 10 files per prompt, and image files up to 50MB on paid plans.
5
Name what you attach in the prompt, for example: use cards.tsx for every list item.
