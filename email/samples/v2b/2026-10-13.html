>  Tool to master
Teach the Framer agent how your team works with Skills
Skills are saved instructions the Framer agent follows across tasks: your design system, your voice, your launch checks. Five moves to make them pay off:
1
Start with a task you repeat every week, such as building a new page from your components.
2
Write the skill with a name, when to use it, what it should produce and the rules to follow.
3
Point at real parts of the project with @pages, @components and @styles.
4
Say what to avoid, for example: never invent new colours or text styles.
5
Run it with its command, like /brand-voice, then refine the rules from what comes back.
 Our take  Skills travel with a project when it is remixed, so a template can carry its own house rules.
