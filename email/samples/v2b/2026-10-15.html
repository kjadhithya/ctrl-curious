>  Case study
Google Play and Android for Cars
Google Play redesigns its store for car displays
Car screens range from wide strips to tall portrait panels. The new store uses one adaptive layout: a navigation rail on landscape screens, a top bar on portrait ones, one-tap installs from the home page, and entertainment for parked use kept apart from apps for driving.

>  Decision toolkit
Navigation rail or top bar?
Google Play's car store uses both, chosen by the shape of the screen. The same call comes up on tablets, foldables and desktop apps.
Navigation rail when
—  The screen is wider than it is tall
—  There are three to seven destinations
—  Content needs the full height
 
Top bar when
—  The screen is tall and narrow
—  There are only a few destinations
—  Content scrolls vertically
Seen in the wild
Google Play for cars
A side rail on landscape dashboards, moving to a top bar above the app cards on portrait ones.
Rule of thumb  Put navigation along the screen's short side so content keeps the long one.
